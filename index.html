Zuri Live

Your coach can see you now.

Turn your phone on its side, prop it up and train. Zuri watches through the camera, counts every rep, times every hold and tells you what to fix. No tapping.

1Body scan. About 12 minutes of simple tests: reach, fold, squat, push-ups, hollow hold, tuck L-sit and a handstand or pike hold.
2Your plan. Built from what the scan finds, aimed at your handstand, your L-sit and a lean, strong body.
3Train hands-free. Targets move up as you earn them. Rescan every 4 weeks.

The camera feed stays on this phone. Nothing is uploaded. Your scans and training log are saved on this phone too.

Tip: on iPhone, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen” so Zuri opens full screen like an app.
